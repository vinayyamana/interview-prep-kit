How we hire

Our hiring process has four stages and usually takes two to three weeks from first call to offer.

  1. Recruiter call (30 min) to discuss your background and what you want next.
  2. Take-home assignment (3 hours) where you build a small payments API.
  3. System design interview (60 min) with two senior engineers.
  4. Team and values conversation (45 min) with your future manager.
